Our story

A little Persian fruit culture, made here.

PariCerise brings together the bright, tart fruit flavours of Persian snacks and preserves with a softer European pantry aesthetic. The result should feel personal, handmade, healthy-looking and joyful — never corporate and never overly polished.

The best version of this brand is warm and sensory: ruby fruit, cream paper, pistachio green, saffron gold, ceramic dishes, linen and subtle rug-inspired patterns.
