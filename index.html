The story

A little nostalgia,
made in Lausanne.

PariCerise grew from the flavours I grew up with: tart fruit, homemade preserves and lavashak. The lavashak is made with no added sugar, while the preserves are sweetened more gently with honey and lemon instead of relying on lots of refined sugar. Everything is prepared in small batches and shared locally, with miniature-like florals and carpet-inspired motifs woven quietly into the visual world.
