The idea behind PariCerise

A small taste of home, made here.

PariCerise brings together Persian fruit traditions and the pleasure of handmade preserves. Lavashak is bold, sour and nostalgic; the jams are slower, softer and made for everyday breakfasts and gifts.

Everything is prepared in small batches, with flavours changing as fruits come into season. For now, orders are arranged directly for local pickup.
